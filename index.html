Welcome to the School

A caring school for positive change

Darel Hikmah International Academy was established in January 2011 and is located at No. 66 Zone C Sokale, Duste Alhaji, Abuja, FCT, Nigeria. The school follows the Approved Curriculum and combines Western education with Islamic studies, so that pupils grow academically, morally and spiritually.

The school offers Nursery, Basic Classes and Senior Classes, alongside Islamiyyah classes for adults and children and e-learning for learners who prefer to study online.

  • Western education delivered on the Approved Curriculum
  • Islamic education and Islamiyyah classes for all ages
  • Small classes that support each child's progress
  • Activities, educational visits and a school playground
