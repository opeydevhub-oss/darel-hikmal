The school portal

One place for pupils, parents, teachers and the school office to follow learning, attendance, results, fees and applications. Choose the portal that belongs to you below.

What works and what does not

The portal sign-in is wired to Supabase Authentication, and dashboard data is read from the secured database. Live access depends on the school project's migrations, security policies and user accounts being configured correctly. The preview information on this overview page is illustrative; do not enter credentials until the school confirms the portal is ready.

How It Will Work

Secure access for every school role

The sign-in flow uses Supabase Authentication. After sign-in, the portal checks the role and active status attached to the account before opening the matching dashboard. The school must provision accounts and verify database security before launch.

  • Students, parents, staff and administrators use their registered email and password.
  • The account's server-side profile determines which dashboard opens.
  • Database Row Level Security controls which records each account can access.
  • The school office provides and manages account access.
