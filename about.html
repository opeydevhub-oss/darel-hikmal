About the School

Darel Hikmah International Academy is a private school in Duste Alhaji, Abuja, founded in January 2011 to provide Western and Islamic education of the highest possible standard.

TEACHERS_AND_STUDENTS_PHOTO.jpg
Who We Are

Established January 2011

The school was established in January 2011 at No. 66 Zone C Sokale, Duste Alhaji, Abuja, FCT, Nigeria. It serves children from the creche and kindergarten through Nursery 1 to 3 and Basic 1 to 5.

Teaching follows the FCT curriculum. Alongside the standard subjects, pupils receive Islamic education, and the school runs Islamiyyah classes for adults and children as well as e-learning classes.

  • Co-educational day school in a quiet Duste Alhaji location
  • Western education on the FCT curriculum
  • Islamic education and Islamiyyah classes for all ages
  • Classrooms, an ICT room and a playground
  • Sports, cultural activities and educational visits

Staff

Staff and teaching team

Class teachers, Islamic education teachers and support staff work together under the leadership team for the welfare of every pupil.

Class teachers

Lead learning across the creche, kindergarten, nursery and basic classes, and follow each pupil's progress.

Islamic education teachers

Teach Islamic education within the school day and lead the Islamiyyah classes for children and adults.

ICT and e-learning staff

Support lessons in the ICT room and help deliver the school's e-learning classes.

Teaching Approach

Curriculum and learning

The school follows the FCT curriculum and combines it with Islamic education so that pupils gain academic knowledge, good character and a sound spiritual foundation.

Lessons are supported by practical activities: pupils use the ICT room, take part in sports and cultural activities, and join educational visits that connect the classroom to real life.
