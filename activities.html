Activities and Educational Visits

Learning at Darel Hikmah International Academy continues beyond the classroom through sports, cultural activities and educational visits.

Sports

Inter-house sports

The school organises inter-house sports so that pupils take part in competition, discipline, teamwork and fair play. Sporting activities also support the school's aim of giving children opportunities for self-development.

  • Pupils represent their class or house
  • Teamwork, discipline and fair play
  • Physical activity alongside classroom learning

Sports events and house activities help pupils build teamwork and school spirit by the school office.

Culture

Cultural activities

Cultural activities give pupils the chance to perform, present and appreciate the traditions of the wider community. They help children become confident speakers, performers and members of society.

  • Performance and presentation practice
  • Appreciation of the culture of Nigeria and beyond
  • Confidence, cooperation and self-expression

Cultural activities give pupils opportunities to appreciate traditions and express their creativity.

Educational Visits

Learning outside the classroom

Educational visits help pupils see how the world around them works, and connect classroom lessons with real life.

Federal Fire Service, FCT Abuja

An educational visit to the Federal Fire Service in Abuja, where pupils learn about fire safety, the work of the fire service and how to respond in an emergency.

Abuja Central Mosque

A visit to Abuja Central Mosque that supports the school's Islamic education and helps pupils understand the place and practice of worship in the community.
