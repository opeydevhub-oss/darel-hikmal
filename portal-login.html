What each portal shows

Every portal has its own layout and its own menu, because each group needs different information.

Student

  • Class information and subject list
  • Attendance record
  • Assignments and learning materials
  • School announcements

Parent

  • Pupil record and class information
  • Attendance records and messages
  • Results and report cards
  • Fees, invoices and application status

Staff

  • Take the register for each lesson
  • Class list and pupil administration
  • Set assignments and share materials
  • Enter continuous assessment marks

Administrator

  • Manage pupils, staff and classes
  • Admissions applications
  • Fees, invoices and payments
  • Announcements and school activities
