Programmes and Classes

Darel Hikmah International Academy offers classes from the creche through Basic 5, together with Islamiyyah classes for adults and children and e-learning classes. Teaching follows the Approved Curriculum.

  • Nursery Classes

  • Senior Classes

Early Childhood

Creche and Kindergarten

The first steps into school life, focused on play, good habits, language and early numeracy.

Creche

Creche

A gentle introduction to school for the youngest children, with supervised play, songs, stories and the beginning of good social habits.

Kindergarten

Kindergarten

Prepares children for nursery work through play-based activities, letters and sounds, counting and creative expression.

Nursery

Nursery 1 to 3

Three nursery classes that build the foundation for primary school.

Nursery 1

Nursery 1

Introductory lessons in literacy, numeracy and Islamic studies.

Nursery 2

Nursery 2

Stronger reading and number work, with more independent class tasks.

Nursery 3

Nursery 3

Consolidation of early skills in preparation for Basic 1.

Primary

Basic Classes

Five basic classes delivering the Approved Curriculum with Islamic education alongside.

Basic 1

Basic 1

Beginning of the basic classes: reading, writing and number work.

Basic 2

Basic 2

Growing independence in classwork across all subject areas.

Basic 3

Basic 3

Wider curriculum coverage with guided practice and group work.

Basic 4

Basic 4

More demanding lessons preparing pupils for the final primary class.

Basic 5

Basic 5

Completion of the basic classes and preparation for secondary school.

Senior Education

Senior Classes (SS1–SS3)

Senior secondary classes for students continuing their education beyond the basic classes. Contact the school office to confirm available subjects, admission requirements and class arrangements.

SS1

Senior Secondary 1

Senior secondary learning and subject development.

SS2

Senior Secondary 2

Continued study and preparation for final secondary-level assessments.

SS3

Senior Secondary 3

Final year of senior secondary education.

For All Ages

Islamiyyah and e-learning classes

Two programmes that extend learning beyond the standard school classes.

Islamiyyah

Islamiyyah for adults and children

Islamic education classes available to school children and to adults who wish to study Islamic subjects.

Class days, time and fees are to be confirmed by the school office.

E-Learning

E-learning classes

Online classes for learners who prefer or need to study from home, supporting the same educational values as the school.

Platform, schedule and enrolment details are to be confirmed by the school office.
