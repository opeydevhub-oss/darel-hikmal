What still needs a backend

This screen is finished as a design but has nothing behind it. To make it work for real, the school would need:

  • Secure sign-in and accounts for each pupil, parent, member of staff and administrator.
  • A database holding pupil records, registers, marks, invoices and applications.
  • Rules that keep each group to its own information only.
  • Payment records kept by the school office, never collected on this website.
  • A record of who changed what, kept by the school.

The screens read their information through one small module (PortalData in js/portal.js), so connecting a real service later means replacing the sample returns and nothing else.
